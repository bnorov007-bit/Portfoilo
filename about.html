The Story

A bit about me.

I have always viewed myself as the sole navigator of my own life, a role that has allowed me to gather a collection of unique stories and unconventional experiences. I’ve learned that life rarely follows a straight line, and while I may not always be the smartest or the most polished person in the room, I find my strength in the quiet chaos of the everyday. I believe that doing what is truly meaningful, rather than what is expected, is the only way to live authentically.

My perspective is deeply rooted in my fascination with people. I often find myself wondering deeply about the invisible threads that connect us, and these reflections have taught me to approach every situation and relationship with a careful, empathetic intentionality. This lifelong curiosity about the 'why' behind human behavior is what drives me forward. It is the reason I aspire to dive into the complexities of Psychology and Neuroscience, seeking to understand the delicate balance between the heart’s emotions and the brain’s intricate wiring.
