The Story

A bit about me.

I have always been the navigator of my own life which is why I think that I have unique stories and unique experiences to share with others. I may not be the smartest or the prettiest but in the chaos of life I try my best by doing what’s meaningful to me.

People and relationships always made me wonder deeply about life and approach situations more carefully. With my continuous curiosity about people, in the future I aspire to study psychology and neuroscience.
